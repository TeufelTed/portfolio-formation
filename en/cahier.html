Competency development workbook

Rotation program leading to the role of account manager, commercial banking.

A development workbook makes progress visible, one behaviour at a time. Participants assess themselves, then their mentor validates: the gap between the two becomes a coaching topic, and each behaviour points to learning nuggets and targeted activities. Aggregated across a cohort, the same statuses show the program lead where the path gets stuck. This is a demonstration: the program, participants and data are fictitious, and your entries stay in your browser.

Browser storage is blocked. The page works normally, but your entries will be lost when you close the tab. Use “Export as JSON” to keep them.

Click a status to change it. My assessment: not demonstrated, partly demonstrated, demonstrated. Mentor-validated: pending, then the same three statuses. Only validated statuses count toward the exit criterion.

The grid aggregates your statuses by capability and level. A demonstrated behaviour counts as 1, partly demonstrated as ½. Click a cell to open that level.

The mentor's or program lead's view: eight fictitious participants with pre-filled statuses. It does not answer “who is behind?” but “where does the path get stuck?”

Where the whole cohort overrates itself

Behaviours for which at least five participants out of eight give themselves a higher status than the one validated by their mentor. When a whole cohort is wrong in the same place, the problem is not individual: the target is misunderstood, or the training presents it poorly.
