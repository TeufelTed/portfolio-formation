Water Loss at Le Mérisier

Scenario Investigation Decision Feedback

You are the claims adjuster on this file

A fitting failed on a water supply riser inside the party wall of unit 302, at Le Mérisier. The water ran for part of the night. The hardwood floor in 302 is swelling, the lower kitchen cabinets are soaked, and the ceiling of unit 202, directly below, is stained over two metres.

The co-owner of 302 calls you: she wants to know who pays for her floor, installed two years ago. The property manager emails you separately. So far, you have no documents on file.

By the end of this scenario, you will be able to

  • choose whom to contact, and in what order;
  • tell common portions, the standard unit and betterments apart, using the syndicate's own documents;
  • allocate the damage between the syndicate's insurance and the co-owner's;
  • determine whether the syndicate's share exceeds its deductible.

Fictitious case, written for this portfolio. The rules shown are those in this syndicate's documents. The deductible changes from one attempt to the next: you will find it in the policy.
